# Edward H. Levi Hall: lock the portal-era reference before first-pass review

This is a reference and existing-evidence cross-check, not a new model readback, modification, or first-pass acceptance.

- [University 125th anniversary, then and now](https://125.uchicago.edu/then-and-now/levi-hall/): the 1948 Administration Building had a central doorway; in 2013 it was converted to an open-air east–west passage, linking Ellis Avenue on the west to Main Quadrangle on the east.
- [Krueck Sexton Partners, portal design](https://ks.partners/projects/edward-h-levi-hall-portal): west entry before/after photographs, retrofit plan and east/west portal views; folding glass walls run alongside the passage to reduce wind. They are not evidence of a transparent panel sealing the through-route. Use the architecture practice's supplied plan and photographs as distinct evidence, not a calibrated survey.
- [University architecture catalog](https://architecture.uchicago.edu/locations/levihall): 1948 building by Holabird, Root & Burgee, 5801 S. Ellis; historic timing and original building credit are separate from the 2013 work. Determine the project's modeled era from its own instructions before editing; do not mix the two doorways.
